Unclutter Zero Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

In short: Unclutter Zero does not collect, store on a server, share or sell your personal data. The app has no internet permission, so it cannot send your photos, videos, files or any other information off your device.

This policy explains how the Unclutter Zero app for Android ("the app") handles information. It applies to the app only.

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. We do not run servers for the app, and we do not receive your files, your file names, your usage, your location or any identifier from your device. The app has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools.

No internet access

The app does not request the Android internet permission (android.permission.INTERNET). Without it, Android does not allow the app to open a network connection. You can check this yourself in Settings, Apps, Unclutter Zero, Permissions. The home screen badge reads the app's own permissions and shows whether this is the case in the version you are running.

Permissions and why the app asks for them

  • Photos and videos (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES and READ_MEDIA_VIDEO, or READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE on Android 12 and earlier): to find screenshots, duplicates, screen recordings and large files on your device and show them to you. The app reads file names, sizes, dates, folder locations and file contents on your device for this purpose only. Nothing is copied off the device.
  • Folders you choose: if you add a folder with Android's folder picker, the app can list and, when you ask it to, delete files in that folder. You can remove that access at any time in Android settings.
  • Notifications (optional, Pro only): if you turn on the weekly duplicate check, the app asks to show a notification on your device when duplicates are found. Notifications are created on your device and are not sent anywhere.

What happens on your device

  • Scanning, duplicate matching, QR code detection and text reading (to spot receipts) all run on your device. QR code and text detection use Google ML Kit models that are bundled inside the app. Because the app has no internet permission, these components cannot transmit data.
  • The app keeps a small amount of information in its own private storage: which items you have already reviewed, the number of deletions you have used today, whether Pro is unlocked, the folders you have added, and your settings. This information stays on your device, is excluded from Android cloud backup, and is removed when you uninstall the app. "Reset reviewed items" in the app clears the review list.
  • When you delete a photo or video, it is moved to Android's system trash, where you can restore it for a limited time. Files in folders you add yourself are deleted permanently, and the app tells you before this happens. The app never deletes anything automatically.
  • The Pro photo compression tool saves smaller copies of your photos in Pictures/Unclutter on your device. Location data is not carried into the smaller copies.

Purchases

Unclutter Pro is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Google Play. The app does not receive or store your payment details. Information that Google shares with developers about purchases, such as order records, is governed by Google's policies, including the Google Privacy Policy.

Sharing

We do not share, sell or transfer your information to anyone, because we do not have it.

Children

The app is a general storage utility and is not directed at children. Because it collects no personal information, it collects none from children either.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy, we will update this policy and change the date at the top. Any change that would add network access or data collection would be described here before it ships.
